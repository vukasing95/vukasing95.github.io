Political and Social Psychologist

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at New York University Abu Dhabi and a Visiting Researcher at ICTA-UAB in Barcelona.

My research examines political psychology and ideology, social and economic systems, intergroup relations, and the ways in which beliefs and social cognition interact with broader societal structures.

Research

My work focuses on political and social psychology, with particular interest in how ideological beliefs and social perceptions are shaped by the broader political, economic, and cultural systems in which people live.

I am especially interested in large-scale cross-national research and in extending psychological research beyond traditionally Western samples.

  • Political Psychology and Ideology
  • Systemic Social Psychology
  • Large-scale Cross-country Research
  • Intra- and Intergroup Relations
  • Belief Systems and Social Cognition
